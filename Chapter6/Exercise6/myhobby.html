Hanging Out with Friends

One of my favorite hobbies in college is hanging out with friends because it gives me a break from classes, homework, and busy schedules. Whether we are grabbing coffee between classes, studying together, going out to eat, or watching movies, I enjoy having time to relax and connect with people who understand what college life is like.

Hanging out with friends also helps me stay balanced and motivated throughout the semester. Being around supportive people helps reduce stress during exams and reminds me to enjoy the college experience, not just focus on academics. These moments create memories that I will always associate with my college years.

A website my friends and I use to find places to hang out is Yelp.
